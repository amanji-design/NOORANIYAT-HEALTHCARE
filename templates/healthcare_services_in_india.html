الأسئلة الشائعة

{% else %}

Frequently Asked Questions

{% endif %}

Can international patients get healthcare treatment in India?

Yes. International patients can seek consultations and treatment in India. Nooraniyat Healthcare can assist with coordination, communication and practical support.

Can I send my medical reports before travelling to India?

Yes. Patients can submit relevant medical reports through the enquiry form so that the required consultation or next steps can be coordinated.

Can you help with a second medical opinion?

Nooraniyat Healthcare can assist patients who want to seek an additional specialist opinion and coordinate the relevant consultation.

Do you help international patients with medical travel?

Yes. We provide coordination and practical support related to medical travel, including communication, treatment coordination and visa-related assistance.

Can you provide an exact treatment cost?

Treatment costs depend on the patient's condition, investigations, specialist, hospital and treatment plan. We can assist with requesting relevant estimates from healthcare providers.

Does Nooraniyat Healthcare itself provide medical treatment?

Nooraniyat Healthcare is a healthcare coordination and patient support service. Medical diagnosis and treatment are provided by qualified doctors and healthcare providers.
