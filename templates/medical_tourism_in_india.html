{% if lang == 'ar' %} تكلفة العلاج والسفر الطبي في الهند {% else %} Medical Treatment Cost and Medical Travel in India {% endif %}

{% if lang == 'ar' %}

تختلف تكلفة العلاج في الهند حسب التشخيص ونوع العلاج والمستشفى والطبيب والفحوصات ومدة الإقامة والحالة الطبية للمريض. لذلك لا يمكن تحديد سعر واحد لجميع المرضى.

يمكن للمرضى إرسال تقاريرهم الطبية للحصول على توجيه أولي وتنسيق الاستشارة المناسبة.

{% else %}

Medical treatment costs in India vary depending on the diagnosis, procedure, hospital, specialist, investigations, length of stay and individual medical condition. There is no single treatment price that applies to every patient.

International patients can send their medical reports for initial guidance and coordination of an appropriate specialist consultation.

Can international patients receive treatment in India?

Yes. International patients can contact hospitals and healthcare providers in India according to their medical needs and applicable visa requirements.

How much does medical treatment in India cost?

Costs vary according to the condition, treatment, hospital, specialist, investigations and length of stay.

Can I send my medical reports?

Yes. Medical reports can be submitted through the enquiry form for assistance with consultation coordination.

Do you provide medical visa assistance?

We provide medical travel and visa coordination support according to the patient's needs and applicable official requirements.

Is Nooraniyat Healthcare a hospital?

No. Nooraniyat Healthcare is a healthcare coordination and support service that helps patients connect with hospitals and specialists.
